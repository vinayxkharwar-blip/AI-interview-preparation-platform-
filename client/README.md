# AI Interview Preparation Platform (Client)

Frontend application for the **AI Interview Preparation Platform**, built with **React**, **Vite**, and **Tailwind CSS**, connecting to a backend powered by **Google Gemini API** (`gemini-3.6-flash`), **Gemini Voice STT**, and **LiveKit WebRTC**.

---

## 🎯 Key Features

1. **Modern Editorial Aesthetic**: Warm cream palette, retro brutalist accents, serif headlines, responsive design, and smooth transitions.
2. **Interactive Live Video Interviews**: LiveKit WebRTC video room integration, camera/mic preview & permissions modal, real-time waveform visualizer, and "I'm Done Speaking" response turns.
3. **Voice Recording & Transcription**: Record answers via browser MediaRecorder, transcribe with backend Gemini Voice STT, review/edit text before grading.
4. **Instant AI Feedback & Scoring**: Rubric-based scoring (1–10), category breakdown, strengths, weaknesses, and improvement suggestions.
5. **Career Hub & Next Best Action**: Resume match analysis, AI cover letter generator, application tracker, and personalized next-step recommendations.
6. **Analytics & Performance Tracking**: Recharts visualization of trends, radar chart competencies, and score progression over time.

---
